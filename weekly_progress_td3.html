TD3: Twin Delayed DDPG

Three Key Innovations

1. Twin Critics

Problem: Single critic overestimates Q-values

$$y = r + \gamma \min_{i=1,2} Q_{\theta_i'}(s', \pi_{\phi'}(s'))$$

Solution: Use minimum of two critics to reduce overestimation bias

2. Delayed Policy Updates

Problem: Policy updated with noisy Q-values

# Update critics every step for i in [1, 2]: update_critic(Q_i) # Update policy every d steps (d=2) if step % d == 0: update_policy(π)

Solution: Let critics stabilize before updating policy

3. Target Policy Smoothing

Problem: Deterministic targets = overfitting

$$\tilde{a} = \pi_{\phi'}(s') + \epsilon$$ $$\epsilon \sim \text{clip}(\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma), -c, c)$$

Solution: Add clipped noise to target actions

TD3 Objective Function

$$\max_\phi \mathbb{E}_{s \sim \mathcal{D}} \left[ Q_{\theta_1}(s, \pi_\phi(s)) \right]$$

Deterministic policy gradient (DPG)

Why TD3 Outperformed

🎯Reason 1: Decisiveness

Deterministic policy = no wasted time on uncertain actions

Once TD3 identifies a good trade setup, it executes immediately and consistently. No hesitation.

⏱️Reason 2: Patience

Delayed updates (d=2) = policy doesn't chase noise

Waiting for critics to stabilize means policy updates are based on reliable Q-values, not fluctuations.

🛡️Reason 3: Robustness

Twin critics + target smoothing = conservative value estimates

Taking min of two critics prevents overoptimistic Q-values that lead to risky trades.

The "Patient Expert" Hypothesis

TD3 combines the best of both worlds:

  • From SAC: Off-policy learning + high sample efficiency
  • From PPO: Conservative updates + stability
  • Plus its own: Deterministic execution + delayed decisions

Result: A "patient expert" that waits for high-confidence setups, then executes decisively without second-guessing.

Unexpected Insights

💡 Insight 1

Deterministic > Stochastic for Trading

Trading rewards consistency and confidence. Randomness in policy adds unnecessary variance.

💡 Insight 2

Delayed Updates = Better Decisions

Waiting for value estimates to stabilize leads to higher-quality policy improvements.

💡 Insight 3

Twin Critics Reduce Risk

Taking pessimistic (min) Q-value estimate prevents overconfident trades that lead to losses.
